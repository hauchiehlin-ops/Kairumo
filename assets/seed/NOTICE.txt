Stroke-order data in the preloaded "Kairumo手冊" notebook
==========================================================

The hand-written Chinese text in the preloaded notebook "Kairumo手冊" is drawn from the stroke
centre lines of the Make Me a Hanzi project (https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi,
graphics.txt), which is derived from the fonts Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai.

Those data are licensed under the Arphic Public License, copyright (C) 1999 Arphic Technology
Co., Ltd. The unaltered licence text is in ARPHICPL.TXT next to this file.

Modification notice (Arphic Public License, section 2a):
  2026-10-06  Kairumo project. From graphics.txt (commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92,
  SHA-256 a28c478b5178e98f67f510b2d52fde08a69dc664654ef43498253b9b764d46ee) only the "medians"
  (stroke centre lines) of the 229 characters used in the notebook (Traditional and Simplified Chinese editions) were kept; glyph outlines were
  dropped. The notebook's strokes (kairumo-manual-ink.json and kairumo-manual-ink-zhHans.json) are those centre lines, scaled,
  slightly perturbed to look hand-written, and drawn as pen strokes. The reduced data are
  available at third_party/makemeahanzi/strokes-subset.json in the Kairumo source tree, and
  remain freely available under the Arphic Public License.
